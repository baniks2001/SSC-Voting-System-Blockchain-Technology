## System Overview

### What is the SSC Voting System?
The SSC Voting System is a secure, blockchain-based electronic voting platform designed for Student Council elections. Your vote is securely recorded on the blockchain, ensuring it cannot be altered or tampered with.

### Key Features
- **Secure Login**: Protected access with your Student ID and password
- **Blockchain Security**: Your vote is permanently and securely stored
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easy-to-use voting system
- **Real-time Validation**: Instant confirmation of your vote
- **Mobile Compatible**: Vote from any device with internet access

### Why Blockchain Voting?
- **Tamper-Proof**: Once cast, your vote cannot be changed
- **Transparent**: The system can verify vote integrity
- **Secure**: Advanced cryptographic protection
- **Anonymous**: Your vote choices remain private

## Getting Started

### System Requirements
- **Device**: Computer, tablet, or smartphone
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions)
- **Internet**: Stable internet connection
- **JavaScript**: Must be enabled in your browser

### Accessing the Voting System
1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to the voting system URL provided by your school
3. You will see the Student Voting Portal login page

### Understanding the Login Screen

#### Status Indicators
The login page shows the current voting status:
- **🟢 Voting Active**: You can login and vote now
- **🟡 Voting Paused**: Voting temporarily suspended
- **🔴 Voting Has Ended**: The election is over
- **⚪ Voting Not Started**: Voting hasn't begun yet

#### Login Credentials
You will need:
- **Student ID**: Your official school student ID number
- **Password**: Your assigned voting password

## The Voting Process

### Step 1: Login

#### Entering Your Credentials
1. **Student ID Field**: 
   - Enter your complete Student ID (e.g., 2023-1234)
   - Use the exact ID provided by your school
   - No spaces or special characters unless in your official ID

2. **Password Field**:
   - Enter your assigned voting password
   - Passwords are case-sensitive
   - Click the eye icon to show/hide password

3. **Sign In Button**:
   - Click to submit your credentials
   - Wait for the system to verify your information

#### Login Issues and Solutions

**"Account Not Found" Error:**
- Double-check your Student ID spelling
- Ensure you're using the correct ID format
- Contact your administrator if your account isn't found

**"Invalid Password" Error:**
- Check password spelling and case sensitivity
- Use the password provided by your school
- Try the "Show Password" option to verify entry

**"Account Inactive" Error:**
- Your account may be deactivated
- Contact your election administrator
- Verify your eligibility to vote

**"Account Locked" Message:**
- Wait for the countdown to complete (usually 10 seconds)
- Try again with correct credentials
- Multiple failed attempts trigger temporary lockout

#### Successful Login
If your credentials are correct and voting is active, you will be automatically redirected to the voting interface.

### Step 2: Understanding the Voting Interface

#### The Voting Dashboard
After successful login, you'll see:
- **Welcome Header**: Shows your voting progress
- **Candidate Sections**: Organized by position/office
- **Selection Panel**: Shows your voting progress
- **Navigation**: Easy access to all voting options

#### Voting Progress Indicator
- **Selected X of Y**: Shows how many positions you've voted for
- **Progress Bar**: Visual representation of your completion
- **Status Messages**: Guidance on your voting progress

## Understanding the Ballot

### Position Structure

#### What are Positions?
Positions are the offices you can vote for, such as:
- **President**: Student council leader
- **Vice President**: Assistant to the President
- **Secretary**: Records meeting minutes
- **Treasurer**: Manages student council finances
- **Senators**: Student representatives

#### Position Information
Each position shows:
- **Position Name**: The office title
- **Selection Limit**: How many candidates you can choose
- **Optional/Required**: Whether you must vote for this position
- **Current Selection**: How many candidates you've selected

### Candidate Information

#### Candidate Cards
Each candidate displays:
- **Photo**: Candidate picture (if available)
- **Name**: Full candidate name
- **Party/Affiliation**: Political party or group
- **Selection Status**: Whether you've selected this candidate

#### Candidate Selection
- **Click to Select**: Tap or click on a candidate to select them
- **Visual Feedback**: Selected candidates are highlighted
- **Selection Limits**: System prevents over-selection
- **Deselect Option**: Click again to remove selection

### Voting Rules and Limits

#### Single Selection Positions
Most positions allow only **one candidate** to be selected:
- President: Select exactly 1 candidate
- Vice President: Select exactly 1 candidate
- Secretary: Select exactly 1 candidate
- Treasurer: Select exactly 1 candidate

#### Multiple Selection Positions
Some positions may allow **multiple candidates**:
- Senators: Select up to the specified number
- Council Members: Multiple selections allowed
- The system will show the maximum allowed

#### Optional Voting
- **You may leave positions empty** if you choose
- **No Selection Option**: Available for every position
- **Partial Voting**: You don't have to vote for every position
- **Empty Selections**: Treated as "abstain" for that position

## Vote Review and Submission

### Step 3: Review Your Selections

#### Accessing the Review Screen
1. **Desktop**: Click "Review & Cast Your Vote" button
2. **Mobile**: Click "Review" button in the action panel
3. **Automatic**: System may prompt review when all positions are viewed

#### Review Screen Features
The review screen shows:
- **Selected Candidates**: All your chosen candidates
- **Empty Positions**: Positions you chose not to vote for
- **Final Confirmation**: Last chance to change selections
- **Submission Options**: Cast your vote or go back

#### Understanding the Review Layout

**Position-by-Position Breakdown:**
- Each position shows your selected candidate(s)
- Empty positions show "No Selection Made"
- Candidate photos and names for confirmation
- Party affiliations displayed

**Change Options:**
- **Back to Voting**: Return to modify selections
- **Confirm Vote**: Proceed to cast your ballot
- **Cancel**: Exit without voting (can log back in)

### Step 4: Casting Your Vote

#### Final Confirmation
Before your vote is submitted:
1. **Verification Screen**: Shows all your selections
2. **Blockchain Notice**: Explains secure vote storage
3. **Final Confirmation**: One last chance to review
4. **Submit Button**: Cast your vote permanently

#### Vote Submission Process
When you click "Cast Vote":
1. **Ballot Creation**: System creates your digital ballot
2. **Blockchain Recording**: Vote is recorded on blockchain
3. **Transaction Confirmation**: System verifies recording
4. **Success Message**: Confirmation that your vote was cast

#### What Happens During Submission
- **Secure Processing**: Your vote is encrypted and secured
- **Blockchain Storage**: Permanently recorded on blockchain
- **Voter Status**: Your account is marked as "voted"
- **Receipt Generation**: Digital receipt created (optional)

## After Voting

### Step 5: Vote Confirmation

#### Success Screen
After successful voting, you'll see:
- **Success Message**: "Vote Successfully Cast"
- **Blockchain Verification**: Confirmation of secure storage
- **Thank You Message**: Appreciation for participation
- **Logout Option**: Securely exit the system

#### Vote Receipt Information
- **Transaction ID**: Unique blockchain transaction identifier
- **Timestamp**: When your vote was recorded
- **Verification Status**: Confirmation of successful recording

### Understanding Vote Security

#### Blockchain Protection
Your vote is protected by:
- **Cryptographic Security**: Advanced encryption methods
- **Decentralized Storage**: Distributed across multiple nodes
- **Immutable Record**: Cannot be altered after submission
- **Transparent Verification**: Can be audited without revealing choices

#### Privacy Protection
- **Secret Ballot**: Your specific choices remain private
- **Anonymous Counting**: Results show totals, not individual votes
- **Secure Storage**: Protected from unauthorized access
- **Audit Trail**: System can verify integrity without revealing votes

### Logout and Session Management

#### Automatic Logout
- **After Voting**: System automatically logs you out
- **Session Timeout**: Inactivity triggers automatic logout
- **Security Measure**: Prevents unauthorized access

#### Manual Logout
- **Anytime**: You can logout before voting
- **Lost Selections**: Logging out without voting loses selections
- **Return Option**: You can log back in to continue voting

## Troubleshooting

### Common Login Issues

#### Problem: Cannot Login
**Possible Causes:**
- Voting is not currently active
- Your account is not registered
- Incorrect Student ID or password
- Account is inactive or locked

**Solutions:**
1. Check the voting status indicator
2. Verify your Student ID with your school
3. Use the exact password provided
4. Wait 10 seconds if account is locked
5. Contact your administrator for account issues

#### Problem: "Account Not Found" Error
**Solutions:**
- Double-check your Student ID spelling
- Try different ID formats (with/without hyphens)
- Confirm you're registered to vote
- Contact the election administrator

#### Problem: "Invalid Password" Error
**Solutions:**
- Check password spelling and capitalization
- Use the password exactly as provided
- Click the eye icon to verify password entry
- Request password reset from administrator

### Voting Interface Issues

#### Problem: Cannot Select Candidates
**Possible Causes:**
- You've reached the selection limit for that position
- The position is optional and you're trying to select too many
- System is processing your previous selection

**Solutions:**
1. Check the selection limit for the position
2. Deselect other candidates if needed
3. Wait for system to process selections
4. Refresh the page if problems persist

#### Problem: Review Button Not Working
**Solutions:**
- Make sure you've viewed all positions
- Try scrolling to see all options
- Check if voting is still active
- Refresh the page and try again

#### Problem: Page Not Loading Correctly
**Solutions:**
- Refresh your browser (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Check your internet connection

### Vote Submission Issues

#### Problem: Vote Submission Failed
**Possible Causes:**
- Internet connection interruption
- System processing delay
- Blockchain node issues

**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Wait a moment and try again
3. Return to voting and try resubmitting
4. Contact administrator if problem persists

#### Problem: System Error During Voting
**Solutions:**
- Take a screenshot of the error message
- Note the time and what you were doing
- Log out and log back in
- Contact technical support with details

### Browser and Device Issues

#### Recommended Browsers
- **Chrome**: Latest version (recommended)
- **Firefox**: Latest version
- **Safari**: Latest version (Mac/iOS)
- **Edge**: Latest version (Windows)

#### Mobile Device Issues
**Solutions:**
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection
- Use the latest version of your mobile browser
- Try rotating your device (portrait/landscape)
- Close other apps to free up memory

#### Slow Performance
**Solutions:**
- Close unnecessary browser tabs
- Clear browser cache
- Restart your device
- Try during off-peak hours

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Can I vote more than once?**
A: No. The system records when you vote and prevents duplicate voting.

**Q: Can I change my vote after submitting?**
A: No. Once your vote is recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be changed.

**Q: Is my vote secret?**
A: Yes. Your specific vote choices remain private. Only totals are shown in results.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: You can review and change your selections before final submission.

**Q: Can I vote from my phone?**
A: Yes. The system works on smartphones, tablets, and computers.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What happens if the internet goes down while I'm voting?**
A: Your selections are saved temporarily. You can log back in to continue voting.

**Q: How do I know my vote was counted?**
A: You'll receive a success confirmation with transaction details.

**Q: Is the system secure?**
A: Yes. The system uses blockchain technology for maximum security.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Contact your election administrator for password assistance.

### Election Process Questions

**Q: When can I vote?**
A: You can vote only when the poll status shows "Voting Active."

**Q: Do I have to vote for every position?**
A: No. All positions are optional. You can leave any position empty.

**Q: How many candidates can I select?**
A: Each position shows the maximum number you can select.

**Q: What if I don't like any candidates?**
A: You can choose "No Selection" for any position.

**Q: Can I see the results?**
A: Results are typically shown after the voting period ends.

### Security Questions

**Q: Can someone find out how I voted?**
A: No. Your vote remains private and anonymous.

**Q: What is blockchain voting?**
A: It's a secure method that records votes permanently and transparently.

**Q: Can the system be hacked?**
A: The blockchain makes it extremely difficult to tamper with votes.

**Q: How are votes protected?**
A: Through encryption, decentralization, and cryptographic security.
